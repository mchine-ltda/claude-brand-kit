# Kiln · brand brief

*A fictional brand, made to show what `/brand-identity` does. These are the example answers given in the interview.*

## Round 1 · The idea
- **What is it?** A shop or venue: a small ceramics studio that sells what it makes.
- **Who is it for?** People in Galway who buy gifts that mean something and want everyday things made by hand.
- **Where?** Galway, Ireland, with a small online shop.
- **Stage:** Launching soon.

## Round 2 · Vision and goals
- **In 3 years:** One place people love: a studio people visit on purpose, plus online sales across Ireland.
- **The problem:** Shop-bought cups feel anonymous; handmade ones are hard to find outside craft fairs.
- **Why us:** You can walk in and watch your cup being made.
- **First goal:** Get found and get the first customers.

## Round 3 · Personality
- **Three words:** warm · honest · local
- **Voice:** warm and friendly
- **Admired:** small makers who show their process. **Never:** glossy luxury homeware.
- **At heart:** dark and dramatic (the kiln at night).

## Round 4 · Specifics
- **Name:** Kiln (already chosen).
- **Colours:** loves terracotta and warm browns; avoid pastel pink.
- **Seen most on:** packaging/products · social media · signage/shopfront.
- **Story:** the arched door of the kiln, glowing when it's firing.

## Choices
- **Visual direction:** A · Fired earth (Clay, Bisque, Char, Ember · Space Grotesk + Fraunces)
- **Logo:** concept 3 · the kiln's arched door with the fire inside, beside the lowercase wordmark
- **Applications (proposed by Claude, kept as is):** moodboard — storefront · shipping box · the product (a glaze-dipped cup and bowl) · seal sticker · shopping bag; in use — clay close-up · class poster · business cards · studio tee
- **Mockups:** coded and stylised (no image generator connected)
