# Kiln · brand strategy (v1.0)

*A fictional brand, made with Claude Brand Kit.*

- **Purpose:** To bring handmade ceramics into everyday mornings.
- **Vision (3 years):** A studio and shop in Galway that people visit on purpose, and a small online shop shipping across Ireland.
- **Mission:** We throw, glaze and fire small batches of cups and bowls, and teach people to make their own.
- **First customer:** Aoife, 34. Works in Galway city, buys gifts that mean something, follows makers on Instagram; wants a cup she'll reach for every morning.
- **Positioning:** For people in Galway who want everyday things made by hand, Kiln is the ceramics studio that makes cups worth keeping, because every piece is thrown and fired in a room you can visit.
- **Promise:** Made by hand, for every morning.
- **Values:** Made by hand · Made for every day · An open door
- **Personality:** warm · honest · local
- **Pillars:** Handmade · Everyday · Open door
- **Headlines:** Handmade cups for people who love their mornings. · Ceramics made in Galway, for every day. · Learn to make the cup you'll drink from.
